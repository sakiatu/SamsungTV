Using your remote

Typing in YouTube

Open Search on the TV, then select letters with the directional pad and OK.

Computer keyboard shortcuts

With the ABC panel open: letters type, Space inserts a space, Backspace/Delete erases, and Enter searches. First tap or type the highlighted TV key to sync. Typing is queued while the TV navigates. Outside the ABC panel: arrows navigate, Enter selects, Backspace goes back, + / − changes volume, M mutes, and digits send numbers.

Connecting your TV

Tap Samsung at the top of the remote to connect or change settings. Keep both devices on the same network and select Allow on the TV when prompted.

Power and apps

Power works while connected; waking a fully powered-off TV isn’t supported. The grid beside YouTube opens your app list.
